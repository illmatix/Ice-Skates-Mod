# Creative Tools

## Ice Rink Creator

A creative-mode item that instantly generates a regulation-sized ice rink with boarded walls.

### Usage

1. Switch to creative mode
2. Find "Ice Rink Creator" in the creative inventory
3. Hold the item and right-click on the ground where you want the rink centered
4. A 30×15 block ice rink with 2-high oak plank boards spawns instantly

### Details

- **Rink size:** 30 blocks long (Z) × 15 blocks wide (X)
- **Surface:** Lake ice blocks
- **Boards:** 2-high oak plank walls around the perimeter with chamfered (open) corners
- **Feedback:** Splash sound effect + chat message on creation
- **Side:** Server-only — the rink is placed on the server; works in singleplayer and multiplayer

### Notes

- The rink replaces whatever blocks are at the target location, so pick an open, flat area
- The rink is centered on the block you click
- Boards use directional oak plank variants (NS/WE) for correct wall orientation, with a fallback to generic planks if directional variants aren't available
