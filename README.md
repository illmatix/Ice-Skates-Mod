# Ice Skates Mod for Vintage Story

Forge skate blades, strap them up, line them for warmth, sharpen them at your workshop — then glide across frozen lakes!

## Features

- **11 blade metals** across 4 progression tiers — from bone to steel, with configurable bridging metals
- **3 strap types** (rawhide, leather, fur) that affect off-ice mobility and hunger
- **4 lining upgrades** for warmth and hunger reduction — applied post-craft, no recipes needed
- **Sharpening system** — portable whetstone for field repairs, workshop jig for heavy maintenance
- **Ice physics** — speed boost on ice, durability drain, particle effects
- **Fully configurable** — toggle individual metals on/off to match your server's progression style

## Quick Start

1. **Forge blades** — smith metal on an anvil (or craft bone blades in the grid)
2. **Assemble skates** — combine blade + strap material + sticks in the crafting grid
3. **Add lining** (optional) — hold skates + lining material, right-click to upgrade
4. **Skate!** — equip in foot slot and step onto ice

## Installation

Drop the `IceSkates` folder into your `VintagestoryData/Mods/` directory.

## Configuration

All blade metals can be individually toggled on or off via `ModConfig/IceSkatesConfig.json`. Compatible with ConfigLib for in-game editing. See [docs/configuration.md](docs/configuration.md) for full details.
